Seven-segment display duplicates digits when entering a number on the keypad.

Steps to reproduce:
  1. Power on the board.
  2. Press key '1' on the keypad.
  3. Press key '2' on the keypad.
  4. Observe the display.

Expected: Display shows "12".
Observed:  Display shows "112" or "1112". The first digit ('1') appears
           two or three times before '2'.

Notes:
  - The duplication is not always the same count; sometimes it's 2 extra
    digits, sometimes 1.
  - The problem is worse when pressing keys quickly.
  - Powering off and on does NOT clear the issue — it is reproducible every run.
